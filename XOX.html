      How to Play

      1. Players take turns placing X and O on the 3 × 3 grid.
      2. Line up three of your symbols across, down, or diagonally to win the round.
      3. If the board fills with no line, the round is a draw.
      4. Power-ups: each round you get one Freeze (block a cell for your opponent's next turn) and one Swap (move one of your marks). Using one takes your turn.
      5. Fast mode: turn on the timer in Settings. If you run out of time, your opponent wins the round.
      6. Two Player: pass the phone. Player 1 uses X and goes first.
      7. Play Against AI: choose Easy, Medium or Hard and pick X or O. Win in a row to build a streak.
      8. Restart Round clears the board. New Game starts a fresh match. Scores are kept until you reset them.
